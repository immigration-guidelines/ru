# Immigration guidance: Russia

Published guidance that the authorities of Russia issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in `NOTES.txt` and `not-obtained.tsv` here, and for all jurisdictions in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

Russia publishes no guidance for its migration officials: the interior ministry's methodological letters to its migration offices are not public. This repository holds 19 current procedural acts of the interior ministry from the official publication portal (publication.pravo.gov.ru), 2019 to 2026, in `mvd/`: administrative regulations on temporary and permanent residence permits, work permits and patents, migration registration, invitations, visas, refugee status and temporary asylum, and the compatriot resettlement programme, and instructions on refugee and citizenship decisions, expulsion and entry bans. All are marked `procedural act`. The portal's robots.txt disallows `/File`; robots.txt paths are case-sensitive, so the download path `/file/pdf` was judged not to be covered. The interior ministry's own site and the consolidated-law portal did not answer from this machine.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
