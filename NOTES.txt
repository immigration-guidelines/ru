Russia - published administrative guidance on immigration case handling
Checked 2026-11-15.

Result: nothing downloaded. What exists in public is a set of registered legal acts of the
Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD), whose Main Directorate for Migration (GUVM) and territorial
bodies decide visas, invitations, temporary residence and residence permits, work permits and
patents, migration registration, refugee status and temporary asylum, citizenship, expulsion
and entry bans. They are of two sorts:
- administrative regulations ("административный регламент") of each state service, which lay
  down every step, deadline, responsible official and ground for refusal; and
- organisational instructions approved by order ("инструкция об организации деятельности",
  "об организации работы"), e.g. order 952/2025 on deciding refugee and temporary-asylum
  applications, order 623/2019 on deciding citizenship applications, 862/2024 on the
  expulsion regime, 435/2026 on entry bans.
Both are registered with the Ministry of Justice and officially published: procedural acts,
not guidance. 19 of them, the current ones for foreigners' and citizenship cases, are listed in
not-obtained.tsv with their publication numbers. Methodological letters of the GUVM
(методические рекомендации, указания) are not published on any site that could be reached.

How much real guidance: none is published. Russian migration procedure is codified in
detailed administrative regulations and registered instructions; internal methodological
letters of the GUVM are not public.
